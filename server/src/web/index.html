📜 ROYAL LUDO RULES
🎲 Roll 6 to Exit Base
Tokens remain protected in your home yard until a 6 is rolled to deploy onto the track.
🌟 Safe Star Havens
The 4 colored starting squares and 4 star-marked tiles are safe. Tokens on these tiles cannot be captured.
⚔️ Capturing & Kill Requirement
Landing on an unprotected opponent sends their token back to yard and awards you a bonus roll! In Classic, at least 1 kill is required to enter your home corridor.
⚡ Bonus Rolls
Rolling a 6 or capturing an opponent token grants an immediate extra roll. Three consecutive 6s pass the turn.
👑 Winning the Match
In Classic & Master, navigate all 4 tokens into the central home triangle. In Quick mode, first to land 2 tokens home wins!
